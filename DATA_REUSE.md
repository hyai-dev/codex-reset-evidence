# Data reuse and attribution

This repository republishes a selected metadata-only projection of a [fixed WhenResets archive snapshot](https://whenresets.com/api/v1/archive?snapshot_id=wr-668dc4b04c0ef700a55c3f8693d603ffe15af7b81bc04cb3fb865037f5f3a822). The authoritative permission statement is carried in `data/manifest.json` under `license`.

WhenResets permits reuse of its authored metadata, classifications and correction notes with attribution to **WhenResets**, the snapshot URL and the `archive-v1` schema version. This does not license original announcements, posts, quoted excerpts, images or third-party datasets. Links to original sources are references, not a claim of rights over their content.

This repository does not assign an MIT license to the data or to the original source material. For the exact terms and exclusions, read `data/manifest.json` and the site's [methodology page](https://whenresets.com/methodology).
